# Model limits and upstream assumptions

The model input is pinned to `alpenlabs/alpen@7f20dcb65fa478c9654626b77a05ea789d28806a`, with `selector_model_version=1` and `deployed_build_confirmed=false`. Version 1 reserves the plan's filter semantics; the selector loop is deliberately not part of steps 1–2.

PLAN.md records three upstream selection generations (M0/M1/M2), the M2 carry pass, reveal headroom and per-input-count pricing. None is inferred to be the production build. This release reports measured complete settlement drain, not simulated future selection. The naive estimate cannot account for fragmentation, future fee/payload/cadence changes, external spends, new top-ups or operator consolidation. It carries no five-day warning guarantee.

EE commits may have OP_RETURN at vout 0, P2TR at vout 1 and optional change. Reveals may have no OP_RETURN. Recognition uses scripts, ownership and outpoints rather than positions. The dust sweeps described at height 969093 are consolidations and are excluded from cost and cadence.

Watch-only descriptor addresses are presumed solvable/spendable by their owner; those wallet flags cannot be observed through Esplora. A wpkh descriptor cannot discover unrelated descriptors, imported keys or a separate P2TR account. Confirm wallet descriptor completeness before operational reliance (Q-21).

Confirmed funding can temporarily fall when a mempool transaction spends the working UTXO; Esplora excludes that input while its change is pending. Pending funds have not disappeared. The planning chart and report use confirmed funding plus recorded pending outputs above 546 sats as one balance, and divide that balance by the recorded spending rate. This assumes pending outputs confirm normally before they are needed; it does not simulate immediate input selection or guarantee a confirmation deadline. The confirmed/pending breakdown remains available in inventory. Historical replay has no archived mempool inventory and retains its original confirmed-chain balance; no pending amounts are invented or interpolated. Stored model fields and historical scores retain their original semantics.

A stable block tip is not a frozen mempool. We reject duplicate outpoints and mark scan start as the observation time, but cannot obtain an atomic mempool snapshot from address-by-address Esplora calls.

## CPFP and funding policy — source review, October 6, 2026

The operator identified [`d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef`](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/commit/d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef) as the deployed revision. Its source was retrieved and reviewed directly, including every Rust file in `crates/btcio` and the exact locked RPC dependency. The deployed identity is operator-supplied, not independently read from running processes. The monitor's original model pin and archived evidence remain unchanged; `deployed_build_confirmed=false` still means the pinned model has not been established as the deployed build.

- **New commit funding requires confirmation.** [Single-envelope funding](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef/crates/btcio/src/writer/builder.rs#L231-L249) and [chunked-envelope signing](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef/crates/btcio/src/writer/chunked_envelope/signer.rs#L80-L101) call `list_unspent(None, None, None, None, None)`. Locked `bitcoind-async-client` **0.10.8** substitutes `min_conf.unwrap_or(1)` in both v29 and v30 clients, consistent with [Bitcoin Core's default](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/30.0.0/rpc/wallet/listunspent/). The signer explicitly leaves zero-confirmation funding as a future consideration because of RBF/CPFP ancestor interactions.
- **No automated CPFP fee-bumping path was found in this revision's writer/broadcaster.** The transactions signal RBF, but that alone does not implement fee replacement. The [fee policy comment](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef/crates/btcio/src/writer/fees.rs#L105-L117) describes incomplete fee-bumping support. RPC bump-fee mocks are test scaffolding, not a production execution path. This does not rule out separate manual operator intervention.
- **Unconfirmed parent-child publication is supported within policy limits.** [The reveal gate](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef/crates/btcio/src/writer/chunked_envelope/handle.rs#L722-L740) allows one reveal while the parent commit is in the mempool if their combined virtual size is below the descendant limit. Larger/multiple reveal cases wait for confirmation. This mechanism is distinct from reusing pending wallet change for new commits or automatically increasing a stuck package's fee.
- **Small-output filtering matches the model threshold.** [The deployed source filter](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/d24ebe2396eecd04201f3d1a4de39fdf8a4827ef/crates/btcio/src/writer/builder.rs#L440-L454) excludes amounts at or below 546 sats. “Excluded from commit funding” describes that policy; “permanently unspendable” would overstate it.

For comparison, the model's September pin `7f20dcb65fa478c9654626b77a05ea789d28806a` and main at `eef78b2299c7fdae379ba58e025f6f3aa289b431` were also reviewed. Those newer revisions have [RBF commit/reveal replacement paths](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/eef78b2299c7fdae379ba58e025f6f3aa289b431/crates/btcio/src/writer/replacement/driver.rs#L654-L699), while ordinary commit funding still requires confirmation. Current main also [accepts exactly 546 sats](https://github.com/alpenlabs/alpen/blob/eef78b2299c7fdae379ba58e025f6f3aa289b431/crates/btcio/src/writer/builder.rs#L698-L707). These newer behaviors must not be attributed to the older deployed revision.

Downloaded source and dependency evidence is saved under ignored `.local/cpfp-review/`; the September pinned builder's SHA-256 matches PLAN.md. No sequencer code, fee policy, wallet transaction or deployed input-selection behavior was changed during this review.

History samples are bounded and coverage-gated. Provider errors retain the last good snapshot. Shallow transaction statuses are rechecked; beyond-finality reorgs and changed frozen history are not fully audited by this reduced release. A replacement or reorg requiring a clean history rebuild can reset only the affected wallet history after investigation; never fabricate current balances. Full archive/reorg audit, fingerprint metrics, quantile confidence intervals and selector equivalence remain future plan work.
