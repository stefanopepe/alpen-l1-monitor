Methodology & open questions

What the data measures

  • Spendable balance includes confirmed usable outputs above 546 sats. Dust is shown separately.
  • Accuracy measures settlement consumption: commit fees, reveal fees and newly stranded outputs. It does not measure the net balance change after deposits.
  • All error comparisons use the same forecast dates across all three models. Negative signed bias means predicted spending was too low.
  • Dates use UTC and reconstructed block times. Historical quotes are shown only if they were observed at the time.

Coverage & unresolved questions

  • Does the selected model remain accurate across more independent periods and different fee conditions?
  • Would output fragmentation stop checkpoint posting before the projected balance reaches zero? The balance projection does not simulate input selection.
  • Funding and wallet actions change the observed balance path. A projected zero is an estimate; chain silence does not prove wallet failure.
Consumption-based exhaustion diagnostic

Recorded at daily forecast origins. Follow-up ends at funding, other wallet actions, an unresolved package or the archive boundary.
